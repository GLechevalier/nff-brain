nff-brain settings

Bring your own AI key

Optional. Without a key (and without a paired local server) captures queue on this device and nothing is sent anywhere. With a key, captured snippets and brain excerpts go directly to the provider below under your own account — nowhere else.

Models

Two slots: a cheap background model distills your captures into brain notes; the chat model answers questions in the DevTools Brain tab. Pick from the list or type any model id.

Prefer everything local? Run nff-brain serve in a project and pair from the extension popup instead — a paired server always takes over, and any notes built here migrate into it automatically.
