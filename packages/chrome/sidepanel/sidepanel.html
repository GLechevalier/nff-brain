    Recorders

    Per-site, off by default: records only actions you perform there. Enabling asks Chrome for that one site; disabling releases it.

      CRM sync

      Adds each person you invite on LinkedIn to your nff-admin CRM (admin.nanoforgeflow.com). Requires the LinkedIn recorder above.

      Record LinkedIn network

      For support only. On a LinkedIn tab, start recording, do the actions (send an invite, open "recently added" connections, send a message), then download the capture and send it over. Stays on this machine.

      Company brain sync

      Shares your brain with the company brain (admin.nanoforgeflow.com), manually or automatically after changes. Nodes you mark private are never sent.

      Record a task

      Records what you do on the current tab into a repeatable workflow. Allow the site first. Stop when done — the recording is turned into a workflow you can replay from the Agent tab.
